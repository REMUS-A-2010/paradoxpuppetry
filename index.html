Our story · Our vision · Our mission

Paradox Puppetry is an independent animation studio creating original stories with heart, atmosphere, and unexpected turns. We bring artists, storytellers, and curious minds together to build worlds worth getting lost in.

Our mission is to make thoughtful, imaginative animation that feels personal, carefully crafted, and impossible to forget.

01

IMAGINE

We start with a question, a character or a world that refuses to leave us alone.

02

BUILD

Writers and artists shape the idea into a story, visual language and living world.

03

ANIMATE

Frame by frame, our team brings every detail, performance and emotion to life.

04

SHARE

We invite our community into the worlds we have made and grow the next one together.
